1. SMS Consent Communication

By voluntarily opting in through the request form, you agree to receive SMS messages from Wollenberg Agency LLC. Your mobile number and SMS consent information are not sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing purposes. See our Privacy Policy for additional information.

2. Types of SMS Communications

Messages relate to your agency opportunity inquiry, follow-up, appointments, and related customer care.

3. Message Frequency

Message frequency varies. You may receive up to 3 SMS messages per week related to your inquiry.

4. Potential Fees

Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

5. Opt-In Method

Consent is provided by checking the SMS consent checkbox before submitting the request form. The checkbox is unchecked by default. Consent is not a condition of purchase or participation.

6. Opt-Out Method

Reply STOP to opt out of SMS messages. After you send STOP, you may receive a confirmation message and will no longer receive messages unless you opt in again.

7. Help

Reply HELP for assistance or contact Wollenberg Agency LLC at (952) 242-0629.

8. Additional Options

If you do not want to receive SMS messages, leave the consent checkbox unchecked. You can still request information without SMS consent.
